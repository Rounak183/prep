## Resources to use

- **Visa sponsor verification:** UK Home Office's public register of licensed sponsors (search "UK register of licensed sponsors" — updated regularly, downloadable list)
- **Referral channels:** LinkedIn 2nd-degree search, alumni networks, company-specific referral portals (if you have any contact at target companies), tech referral communities
- **Comp/process research:** Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Blind — useful once applications are in flight, not before

---

## Why this sequencing, not "finish everything, then apply"

1. FAANG interview loops (application → screen → onsite → offer) commonly take 4-8+ weeks. Waiting until Aug to apply pushes your first real interview into Sept regardless of readiness.
2. Referral outreach and resume feedback loops cost you nothing to start now and actively benefit from lead time.
3. Reserve end-of-July for *volume* of applications, not for the *first* application.
